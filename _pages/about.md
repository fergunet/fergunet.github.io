# This is Pablo Garcia Sanchez's home on the World Wide Web!

I am an Associate Professor (Profesor Titular de Universidad) in the [Department of Computer Engineering, Automation and Robotics](https://icar.ugr.es) at the [University of Granada](https://www.ugr.es) and the current director of the [Free Software Office](https://osl.ugr.es) of the Vice-Rectorate for Digital Transformation. I was one of the organisers of the joint [Evostar conference on Evolutionary Computing](https://evostar.org) from 2014 to 2019, and chair of the EvoApplications conference in 2025 and 2026, and organizer of the Python España Conference (PyConES) 2022. 

My interests include service-oriented computing, evolutionary computing, computational intelligence in video games, distributed algorithms, green computing, free software and open science. I also enjoy cinema and drawing outdoors. Oh, and I have an orange cat named Daisy and another calico cat named Peach.
