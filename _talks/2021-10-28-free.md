In the lecture, I explained the concepts and philosophy behind Free and Open-Source Software and its relation to open science invited by the [Vilnius University Library](https://arqus.ugr.es/noticias/conferencia-free-open-source-software-pablo-garcia/). 

I also discussed the differences between the individual open software licenses. Finally, I explained how the Free Software Office of the University of Granada helps and advises the whole university community in everything related to Open-Source Software.
